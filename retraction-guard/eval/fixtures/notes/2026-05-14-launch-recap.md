# Bluebird launch recap (May 14)

Bluebird launched company-wide on May 6.

- Launched to 1,000+ analysts across finance, ops and marketing.
- Report build time dropped from 2 days to 20 minutes for the first teams on it.
- Weekly actives grew 3x in the first month.
- Biggest surprise: ops analysts adopted faster than finance, because their reports change weekly.
